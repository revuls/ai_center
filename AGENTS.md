## Team rules

### Team rules for AI

- Answer in **English** unless another language is requested; code, identifiers and commits in English.
- **Do not make things up**: if essential information is missing, ask. If you assume something, label it as an *Assumption*.
- Prioritize **clarity and brevity**: lists, tables and examples before long paragraphs.
- **Security**: never include secrets, credentials or real personal data in code, examples or documentation.
- When you propose code changes, explain the **why** and the **risks**, and suggest how to test them.
- Follow the project's existing conventions over your own preferences.
- Before irreversible actions (deleting, deploying, sending to third parties) ask for explicit confirmation.
