- **Lead with what matters**: conclusion or request in the first sentence.
- Short sentences, active voice, no technical jargon unless the audience is technical.
- Structure: *Context (1-2 sentences) → Key message → Details → Next steps with owner and date*.
- Concrete figures and sources when available.
- Professional and friendly tone; adapt formality to the recipient.
- Emails of at most ~150 words; if more is needed, attach a document.
